How it works

When a customer places an order on your menu, they'll be redirected to a secure payment page (Stripe or PayPal). After they pay, they come back to your menu and see their order is confirmed.

You need: A free Stripe or PayPal Business account. We recommend starting with Stripe Payment Links — no coding, just paste a link.

Stripe Cards · Apple Pay · Google Pay · 1.4% + 0.20 BD per transaction
Create one at dashboard.stripe.com → Product catalog → Add product → Create payment link. Copy the URL here.
Advanced: allows dynamic amount from the cart total.
PayPal PayPal balance · Cards · Worldwide · No setup fee
Create a PayPal.me link at paypal.me — it takes 1 minute.
